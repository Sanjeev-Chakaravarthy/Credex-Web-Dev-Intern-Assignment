## Interview 1 — A.K. (Startup Founder)

**Role:** Founder  
**Company stage:** 5-person SaaS startup  
**Interview duration:** 15 minutes  
**Date:** 2026-05-27

### Quotes
- “We probably spend more on AI than we realize every month.”
- “Different engineers use different AI tools and nobody tracks overlap.”
- “I didn’t even know discounted AI credits existed before this.”

### Most surprising insight
The biggest concern was not API pricing — it was duplicate subscriptions across the team.

### What it changed in AISpendLens
I added overlap detection between Cursor, Copilot, Claude, and ChatGPT subscriptions to surface duplicate spending more clearly.

## Interview 3 — P.M. (Developer Intern)

**Role:** Developer Intern  
**Company stage:** Early-stage startup  
**Interview duration:** 10 minutes  
**Date:** 2026-05-27

### Quotes
- “Nobody really knows which AI plans everyone is using.”
- “Most of us expense AI tools individually.”
- “Finance rarely reviews these subscriptions unless costs suddenly spike.”

## Overall Patterns Observed

### Common Problems
- Teams rarely track total AI spend centrally.
- Duplicate subscriptions are common.
- Many users stay on higher plans longer than necessary.
- AI spending visibility is poor across startups.

### Product Insights
- Users want actionable recommendations, not just analytics.
- Visual savings summaries increase trust.
- Shareable reports make the product feel more useful internally.
- Credex positioning becomes valuable only after savings are clearly demonstrated.

### Product Changes Made
- Added overlap detection logic.
- Improved recommendation clarity.
- Enhanced shareable report experience.
- Added stronger savings visualization on the results page.
