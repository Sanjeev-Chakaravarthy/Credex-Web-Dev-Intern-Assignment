## FAQ

### Q: Is this really free?
**A:** Yes. The audit is free forever. We make money through our partnership with Credex — if your savings are significant, we offer to connect you with their procurement optimization service. You're never charged by AISpendLens.

### Q: How do you calculate savings?
**A:** We use official pricing data from each vendor's pricing page, updated monthly. Our engine compares your current plan against all available alternatives for your team size and usage pattern. The math is deterministic — no AI guessing. Every recommendation shows the exact dollar impact.

### Q: Do I need to create an account?
**A:** No. You can run a full audit without signing up. If you want to save your results or get them emailed, we'll ask for your email after you see the results. We never gate the value behind a signup wall.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes. We store audit data in Supabase (SOC 2 compliant PostgreSQL). We don't store your passwords or API keys — we only collect the spend data you input. Shared reports use randomized URLs that can't be guessed. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

### Q: How often should I re-run the audit?
**A:** We recommend quarterly, or whenever you add a new AI tool, change team size, or see a pricing change from a vendor. AI tool pricing changes frequently — what's optimal today might not be in 3 months.

### Q: What is Credex?
**A:** Credex is a procurement optimization platform. They negotiate bulk pricing with SaaS vendors on behalf of startups. If AISpendLens finds significant savings opportunities (>$1,000/year), we'll offer to connect you with Credex for additional procurement-level savings. It's completely optional.

### Q: Can I share my audit results?
**A:** Yes! Every audit generates a unique shareable URL. Share it with your co-founder, CFO, or engineering manager. No login required to view a shared report.

### Q: What if the tool recommends changes I disagree with?
**A:** Our recommendations are suggestions, not mandates. We optimize for cost, but you might have valid reasons to keep a specific tool or plan (compliance, team preference, specific features). Use the audit as a starting point for the conversation, not the final word.

## Copy Principles

1. **Specificity wins.** "$2,800/month" beats "a lot of money." "60 seconds" beats "quick."
2. **Action over description.** "Audit My AI Spend" beats "Learn More."
3. **Pain before solution.** Lead with the problem they feel, then present the fix.
4. **No jargon.** Don't say "optimize your AI procurement stack." Say "stop overpaying for AI tools."
5. **Casual but credible.** Write like a smart friend, not a corporate brochure.
6. **Value before ask.** Every CTA should come *after* demonstrating value.
